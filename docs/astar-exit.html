Temporary Astar XC20 Exit

Owner-only helper to move leftover HDX or USDT XC20 balances from an Astar EVM wallet (chain id 592) via the official XCM precompile. No custody, no approve(), no SoneVibe contracts.

Temporary / unlinked page. DOT is out of scope. You call 0x…5004 directly from your wallet. ASTR is required for gas.

2. Asset & amount

Test amounts are above the destination minimum balance (Hydration: 1 HDX, Asset Hub USDT: 0.01) plus fees. Sending less to an empty account may be lost.
