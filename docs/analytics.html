How APR & APY are calculated

Fee APR = LP fees (7d) ÷ 7 × 365 ÷ TVL
APY = (1 + APR / 365)365 − 1
  • Volume comes from real Swap events on-chain, not estimates.
  • LPs keep their share of the swap fee; the rest goes to the protocol treasury.
  • APY assumes you reinvest fees daily. Impermanent loss is not included.
