How it works

From wallet to first swap in three steps

No account and no deposit into a company wallet. You sign every transaction yourself.

Connect your wallet

Open the app with MetaMask, Trust Wallet or Nova and switch to BNB Chain. You need a little BNB for network fees.

Swap or add liquidity

Pick the tokens and the amount. The app shows the rate, the price impact and your slippage limit before you confirm.

Fees accrue in the pool

Each swap pays 0.25%. Liquidity providers receive their part when they remove liquidity, along with their share of the pool.

Fee APR 7d avg is the average daily swap fee paid to liquidity providers over the last 7 days (or the pool's age, at least 1 day), annualized and divided by the pool's liquidity. It is historical and variable, not guaranteed, and it does not include impermanent loss.

What to know before you deposit

The rules of the protocol, in plain words.

  • 0.25% swap fee. 5/6 goes to liquidity providers and 1/6 to the protocol treasury.
  • Non-custodial. Liquidity sits in the pair contracts and you sign every transaction from your own wallet.
  • Admin roles are disclosed. The docs list which addresses hold them and what they can change. Security and admin controls →
  • Live data. Stats here and in Analytics come from the chain, not from a private database.
  • Lending. Lending is coming soon on BNB Chain.
