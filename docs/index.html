How it works

The rules every user should know before depositing.

  • 0.25% swap fee. 5/6 goes to liquidity providers, 1/6 to the protocol treasury.
  • Oracle-priced lending. Collateral is valued by the SoneVibe oracle; positions below a health factor of 1.0 can be liquidated with a 10% bonus, up to 50% of the debt at a time.
  • Self-custody. Your assets stay in smart contracts you interact with directly from your wallet.
  • Live data. Stats on this page and in Analytics are read from the chain, not from a private database.
